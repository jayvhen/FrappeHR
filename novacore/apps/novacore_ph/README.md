# novacore_ph

NovaCore HRIS custom app for Frappe HR v15 (see `NovaCore Frappe HR – TO-BE System Diagrams/`).

- PH Statutory Engine: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR withholding tax (table driven)
- Night differential and overtime computed from check-ins
- Employee lifecycle stages (Probationary → Regular → Notice Period → Separated)
- 24-hour approval escalation for leave and overtime requests
- Payroll approval workflow (Payroll Officer → HR/Finance Manager)
- HR Helpdesk tickets
- Government reports: SSS R-3, PhilHealth RF-1, Pag-IBIG MCRF, BIR 1601-C
